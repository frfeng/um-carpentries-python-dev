Introduction

This report was prepared to the attention of the United Nations. It analyzes the relationship between a country’s GDP, its life expectancy and CO2 emissions. Our goal is to determine to what degree a country’s economic strength or weakness may be related to its public health status and impact on climate pollution. We hypothesize that both life expectancy and CO2 emissions increase with a country’s GDP.

Life expectancy and GDP

Figure

Code 
import altair as alt

alt.Chart(gapminder_1997).mark_circle().encode(
    x=alt.X('gdpPercap').title('GDP Per Capita'),
    y=alt.Y('lifeExp').title('Life Expectancy'),
    color=alt.Color('continent').scale(scheme='set1'),
    size=alt.Size('pop').title('Population (in millions)'),
    shape='continent',
    tooltip=['country', 
             alt.Tooltip('pop')
             .title('Population in million')
             .format(',.1f')
             ],
).transform_calculate(
    pop='datum.pop/1000000'
).properties(
    title='Do people in wealthy countries live longer?',
    width='container',
).interactive()

Description

Figure 1 shows the relationship between GDP per capita and life expectancy for a total of 142 countries. For this set of countries, economic wealth ranged from a minimum of USD $312 to a maximum of USD $41,283 per capita.
